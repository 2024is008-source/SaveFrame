A TikTok photo slideshow contains separate image files. It is different from a video that happens to show photographs: downloading that video gives you an MP4, not the original individual photos. TikSaveMP4 uses the media returned by the source to show the correct result.

## Choose a photo post

Copy the sharing link from the actual photo post. A direct photo URL includes /@creator/photo/ followed by a numeric ID. An official short share URL is also supported. A screenshot of the post or a creator profile link is not a substitute for the post’s URL.

## Save the available images

1. Open the [TikTok photo downloader](/photo-downloader).
2. Paste the post link and select Download Photo.
3. Check the creator, thumbnails and slide count.
4. Choose a slide to download that image, or use Download All for the ZIP option.
5. Wait for preparation, then open the browser’s download list.

The image format depends on the available source file. JPEG and WebP are supported; an image is not upscaled simply because you save it through the site.

## Keep the slideshow order

Use the complete ZIP when you want the whole post. The archive uses ordered filenames so the photo sequence is easier to preserve after extraction. See [how to open a photo ZIP](/blog/download-tiktok-photos-zip) before importing the images into a gallery app.

## Understand the limits

The ZIP contains the downloadable media files, not a playable reconstruction of the TikTok post. A photo slideshow’s soundtrack is not included as a separate music download. If an image is unavailable or the source blocks access, the site returns an error rather than inventing the missing slide.
