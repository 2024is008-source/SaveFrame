A desktop browser is useful when you want to inspect a TikTok video’s available formats and choose where the completed file is saved. You can use the same TikSaveMP4 tools on Windows and macOS; the browser handles the final download.

## Open the individual post

Find the video you want and copy its post URL. A profile address or a feed page is not a downloadable post. If you have an official short share link, paste that link directly rather than following an unfamiliar third-party link expander.

## Save from the result

1. Open the [TikTok video downloader](/video-downloader).
2. Paste the public post link and inspect it.
3. Confirm the creator and choose an available video format.
4. Select its download button and wait for file preparation.
5. Use the browser’s download list to open the file or reveal its folder.

If your browser is configured to ask where files should go, choose a folder you can find later. Otherwise check its current default download location.

## Verify before organizing

Play the saved MP4 before moving it into a larger collection. Check the picture, audio and ending, especially if your connection dropped during the transfer. Copying a broken file to a backup drive will not repair it.

For your own clips, keep the editing project or camera original alongside the downloaded copy. The platform-delivered video may be compressed and is not a replacement for your source material.

## Work with photo posts

A photo slideshow can be saved as separate images or a ZIP through the [photo downloader](/photo-downloader). Extract the archive before importing images into editing software. Preserve the numbered order if the sequence matters, and follow the [backup checklist](/blog/backup-your-tiktok-content) for longer-term organization.
