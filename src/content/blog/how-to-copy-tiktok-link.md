The downloader needs the address of a specific public TikTok post. Copying the creator’s profile page, a search result or a screenshot will not identify the video or photo slideshow you want. A correct link is the most useful first check when a download fails.

## Copy from the post

Open the individual video or photo slideshow and look for the sharing controls. Copy its link, return to TikSaveMP4 and paste it into the input field. On a computer, the browser address bar is useful when it shows the individual post rather than a feed or profile.

## Recognize supported links

- Video posts use tiktok.com/@creator/video/ followed by a numeric post ID.
- Photo posts use tiktok.com/@creator/photo/ followed by a numeric post ID.
- Official short links use vm.tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com or tiktok.com/t/.

Do not remove the post ID or replace it with the title. Extra sharing parameters are not needed to identify a supported direct post; the server normalizes accepted links before inspection.

## Paste on your device

Use the Paste button if your browser permits clipboard access. If it refuses access, tap the input and use your device’s normal Paste action instead. You do not need to change browser security settings just to enter a link.

## Check the detected tool

A direct video link selects the video tool; a direct photo link selects the photo tool. Short links are resolved on the server, so their final media type may become clear only after inspection. Check the returned preview before downloading.

If the URL points to a private post, profile or live stream, obtain the appropriate public post link instead. Continue with the [video guide](/blog/how-to-download-tiktok-videos-mp4) or [photo slideshow guide](/blog/download-tiktok-photo-slideshows).
