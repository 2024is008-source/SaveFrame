TikSaveMP4 prepares accessible public TikTok videos for your browser. Start with the individual post rather than a profile, search page or live stream. Before saving someone else’s work, make sure you have permission to do so.

## Copy the right post

Open the video you want and use its sharing controls to copy the link. Direct links contain an @creator name, /video/ and a numeric post ID. Official short share links are accepted too; you do not need to expand them yourself.

TikTok also offers its own Save video option when the creator allows downloads. See [TikTok’s download help](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/video-downloads) for that built-in option.

## Prepare an MP4

1. Open the [TikTok video downloader](/video-downloader).
2. Paste the copied link and select Download Video.
3. Check the creator and preview before choosing an available format.
4. Select the result’s download button and wait for preparation to finish.
5. Open your browser’s download list and check the saved file.

The first button inspects the link; the result’s download button requests the actual file. Keep the page open while the server is preparing it.

## Check picture and sound

Play the saved MP4 from beginning to end. A larger resolution choice cannot restore detail missing from the uploaded source, and some sources may not include usable audio. Read the [audio checks](/blog/download-tiktok-videos-with-sound) if the picture plays without sound.

## If the post is unavailable

A public link can still be restricted to signed-in users, certain regions or certain networks. Repeated requests do not remove those restrictions. Follow the [troubleshooting guide](/blog/tiktok-downloader-not-working) and try again later when the service reports a temporary restriction.
